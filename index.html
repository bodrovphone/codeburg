Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about keys, monitors, internet, meetings, and reservations.

What is included in the 10€ daily pass?

Our 10€ daily pass includes full workspace access during operating hours, unlimited specialty coffee & organic teas, gigabit dual-fiber internet (1GB+), 32" 4K monitor (first-come basis), keyboard & mouse if needed, motorized height-adjustable desks, ergonomic mesh chairs, clean filtered water, weekly snacks, and use of all chill and common areas.

How do the 24/7 keys and working hours work?

Our staffed hours are Monday to Friday, 09:30 - 18:00. Day pass visitors work within these hours. However, weekly and monthly members are not restricted by them: you receive your own physical key and RFID access so you can code 24/7, including late nights, early mornings, weekends, and holidays.

Can I take calls and host video meetings?

Absolutely! We are all remote engineers and builders wearing headphones. Normal conversational calls are welcome with headphones. For sensitive conversations like interviews, client demos, or salary discussions, you can reserve and close the sound-isolated chill room (equipped with desk and internet) for up to 1 hour daily.

How does online desk booking work?

We manage all reservations seamlessly through OhMyDesk. The live availability indicator at the top of this page connects directly to the space API so you can confirm open desks in real-time and secure your spot with card payment in seconds.

Is there parking available nearby?

Yes, there are several convenient parking options nearby, including municipal green/blue zone street parking and dedicated paid parking lots within 2-3 minutes walking distance from the entrance.

How do I reach Alex or the community?

Feel free to reach out directly via Telegram, call +359 88 4489289, or message on WhatsApp. We're always happy to show you around the space!
